Project Background

Solar photovoltaic systems suffer from efficiency losses due to changing sunlight and load conditions. This project simulates a complete PV system in Proteus, implements multiple MPPT algorithms, and compares their performance to identify the most efficient solution. It also demonstrates a low-cost Arduino-based MPPT model for educational use.

Objectives

  • Develop a complete PV system simulation with Arduino-based MPPT control.
  • Implement P&O, INC, FOCV, FLC, and VSS-P&O MPPT controllers.
  • Evaluate controller performance under varying irradiance, temperature, and shading conditions.
  • Compare tracking efficiency, convergence speed, stability, robustness, and computational complexity.
  • Demonstrate practical MPPT implementation using a simplified Arduino-based instructional model.
